## Free use

| Situation | Free production use? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal, educational, or research use | Yes | For natural persons. |
| Freelancer or sole trader below the thresholds | Yes | May serve a client of any size on their own Mac. The client does not receive its own license. |
| Organization with fewer than 25 people and under EUR 1,000,000 annual gross revenue or operating budget | Yes | Both limits must be met by the whole controlled group. |
| Evaluation, testing, or development without production use | Yes | Available to organizations of any size under BSL 1.1. |
| Employee using it for a larger employer | No | The relevant use benefits the employer, not the employee personally. |
| Organization crossing either threshold | Temporarily | A 90-day transition period applies after the fiscal year ends. |
| Organization above either threshold | No | Stop production use or contact `686f6c61@00b.tech`. |

Gross revenue is used instead of profit because it is objective and can be
applied consistently. Parent companies, subsidiaries, and entities under
common control are assessed together. Employees and individual contractors
count toward the people limit.

## Freelancers serving large clients

A qualifying independent professional may use Claude Island to perform work
for a large client. That does not grant the client a license to install,
operate, control, copy, or distribute Claude Island. A deployment managed by
the client, installed on client-controlled Macs, or used directly by the
client requires the client's own license.

Claude Island es gratuito para uso personal, educativo, para freelancers y
para organizaciones que cumplan **a la vez** estos dos límites: menos de 25
personas y menos de 1.000.000 EUR de facturación bruta anual o presupuesto.
Se computa todo el grupo empresarial. Las organizaciones que superen un límite
disponen de 90 días después del cierre del ejercicio para dejar de usarlo en
producción o escribir a `686f6c61@00b.tech`.

Un freelance que cumpla los límites puede utilizarlo para prestar servicios a
un cliente grande desde su propio entorno. El cliente no recibe por ello una
licencia para instalarlo o utilizarlo directamente.
