# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project discussions should be respectful, technically constructive and safe
for people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, threats and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

Feedback should address the work rather than the person. Reports, examples and
screenshots must not expose private Claude transcripts, credentials, customer
data or another person's identifying information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, privacy,
security or the project's ability to operate.

Conduct concerns may be sent privately to `686f6c61@00b.tech`. Security
vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are
handled on a best-effort basis and should include links or other evidence.

This policy applies in the repository, project-operated communication spaces,
and public interactions where someone is representing Claude Island.
